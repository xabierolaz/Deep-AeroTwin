## Primary target: Advanced Engineering Informatics

Why it ranks first:

- Exact UPNA workbook row: Elsevier, JCR Q1, best percentile 98, SJR Q1.
- The independent journal scout found official Elsevier indicators of 4 days to
  first decision, 49 days to decision after review, 125 days to acceptance, and
  9 days from acceptance to online publication. These are published indicators,
  not guarantees and Elsevier does not label the statistic/window clearly.
- The knowledge-representation and engineering digital-twin framing can fit if
  SPPA is evaluated as explicit semantic engineering knowledge, not as a visual
  demo or detector paper.

Hard fit condition: the paper must demonstrate knowledge representation,
generality, scalability, and engineering value through non-circular measured
experiments. The current manuscript does not yet satisfy this condition.

Journal page:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/advanced-engineering-informatics

## Strong alternative: Journal of Geovisualization and Spatial Analysis

- Exact official Springer CRUE eligible-journal workbook match at title
  `Journal of Geovisualization and Spatial Analysis`, eISSN 2509-8829,
  publishing model Hybrid.
- Exact UPNA impact row: JCR Q1, percentile 96.8, SJR Q1.
- Official Springer journal page on 2026-07-15: JIF 6.8 (2025), median 6 days
  to first decision, continuous article publishing, hybrid model.
- Scope explicitly includes geospatial analysis/visualization, remote sensing,
  photogrammetry, real/virtual environments, and dynamic phenomena.

Hard fit condition: add a defensible geospatial task and spatial-quality
evaluation. A runtime contract with four qualitative images is not enough.

Journal page:
https://link.springer.com/journal/41651

Springer CRUE agreement:
https://www.springernature.com/gp/open-science/oa-agreements/spain/crue-csic

## Other candidates

- Computer-Aided Design: strongest literal geometry/representation fit, but the
  inspected UPNA JCR row is Q2 even though SJR is Q1.
- ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing: prestigious Q1 and UAV/
  reconstruction fit, but the current evidence bar is far below the journal.
- Robotics and Autonomous Systems: Q1 and covered, but slow official indicators
  and requires demonstrated autonomous-system impact.
- IEEE JSTARS: Q1 in the inspected evidence, but UPNA coverage was reported as a
  20 percent discount rather than full APC coverage.
